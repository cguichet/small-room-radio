Rain Through a Window

Noise box for making sounds inspired by rain pitter pattering on a roof.

The Rain knob generates randomly spaced rain drops with increasing density. Each rain drop is a small blip of voltage.

The blips pass through a low pass filter and a high pass filter (the window). Resonance controls the LPF resonance, and Motion randomly modulates the LPF cutoff with every new blip.

eGuzheng

Eleven analog resonators, D3 to D5 in D major pentatonic, played here by a model of Sound Workshop's bridged-T circuit.

Tap a pad to strike its note: higher on the pad is a harder tap. Touch a drone strip to make that voice drone, softly low on the strip and fully near the top; tap the finger again to lift it. Decay sets how long struck notes ring, Saw leans every voice toward a sawtooth, and dust noise adds crackle or scatters random strikes across the notes. Keys A to ' strike the notes too.
