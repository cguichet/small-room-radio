Rain Through a Window

A noise instrument from Sound Workshop, played here by a model of its circuit. Dust on the glass rings a resonant filter: each blip of noise picks a new pitch, and the filter rings at it like a wind chime.

Pick the weather on the keys, or drag the knobs and faders. Flip the switch on top of the radio back to Radio for the shows.
